## 2. O gardiyanın (John Mark) sonraki tavrı

**KISMEN.**
- **Sertleştiğini ya da sertleşmediğini gösteren bağımsız arşiv belgesi bulamadım.** Haslam ekibi yalnız konuşmanın içini anlatıyor:
  - "The guard repeatedly resists the pressure to engage in the harsh treatment of the prisoners." [SA]
  - "The Guard, John Mark, repeatedly resists this pressure from the Warden over the course of the meeting." [IM]
- **Kendi anlatımı, 2011** (öz beyan) [SM]:
  - "During the day shift, when I worked, no one did anything that was beyond what you'd expect in a situation like that. But Zimbardo went out of his way to create tension. Things like forced sleep deprivation—he was really pushing the envelope."
  - "I just didn't like the whole idea of constantly disturbing people and asking them to recite their prisoner numbers in a count. I certainly didn't like when they put a guy in solitary confinement."
  - "I brought joints with me, and every day I wanted to give them to the prisoners. I looked at their faces and saw how they were getting dispirited and I felt sorry for them."
    - Bu söz uyuşturucu içeriyor. Videoda kullanılırsa reklam uygunluğu açısından tartılmalı.
  - "I felt that throughout the experiment, he knew what he wanted and then tried to shape the experiment—by how it was constructed, and how it played out—to fit the conclusion that he had already worked out."
  - "Based on my experience, and what I saw and what I felt, I think that was a real stretch. I don't think the actual events match up with the bold headline. I never did, and I haven't changed my opinion."
- **2007 tarihli söz, ikincil kaynaktan** [IM]: "My opinion, based on my observations, was that Zimbardo began with a preformed blockbuster conclusion and designed an experiment to 'prove' that conclusion".
  - In-Mind bunu bir Stanford mezun dergisi mektubuna dayandırıyor; özgün mektubu göremedim.
- **Yön notundaki "Zimbardo'yu yönlendirmekle eleştirdi" iddiası:** DOĞRU [SM].
- **"Sertleşmedi" iddiası:** yalnız Mark'ın kendi anlatımına ve konuşmadaki direncine dayanıyor. Senaryoda "kendi anlatımına göre" kaydıyla verilmeli.

### "Rol, doğaçlama, kendi deneyim" sözleri (tam)
- **1971, deneyin son günü,** "warden"a (Guard 4, debriefing) [LT s. 8]:
  - "I thought it would be better for the study if I presented what I thought to be a realistic relationship between guard and prisoner. [. . .] [T]hroughout the entire experiment I was an actor, and I was hamming it up. [. . .] I was role playing."
- **2011** [SM]:
  - "What came over me was not an accident. It was planned. I set out with a definite plan in mind, to try to force the action, force something to happen, so that the researchers would have something to work with. After all, what could they possibly learn from guys sitting around like it was a country club? So I consciously created this persona."
  - "I was kind of running my own experiment in there, by saying, 'How far can I push these things and how much abuse will these people take before they say, "knock it off?"' But the other guards didn't stop me. They seemed to join in. They were taking my lead. Not a single guard said, 'I don't think we should do this.'"
- **2017–2018** [BL; Vox ve IHE aynı sözü aktarıyor]:
  - "I took it as a kind of an improv exercise. I believed that I was doing what the researchers wanted me to do, and I thought I'd do it better than anybody else by creating this despicable guard persona."
  - Deney sonrası, Zimbardo'nun tebriki üzerine: "As I was walking down the hall, he made it a point to come and let me know what a great job I'd done. I actually felt like I had accomplished something good because I had contributed in some way to the understanding of human nature."
- **2024** [LP]:
  - "He wanted to present me as this 'evil guard' figure, even though he directed many of my actions."
  - "Right after the experiment, he came over and congratulated me, saying I'd done a fantastic job."

### Pişmanlık ve "yaptıklarım gerçekti" anlamında söz
**KISMEN.**
- Pişmanlık sözü **DOĞRU**, 2011 [SM]:
  - "The fact that I ramped up the intimidation and the mental abuse without any real sense as to whether I was hurting anybody? I definitely regret that. But in the long run, no one suffered any lasting damage."
- Blum'un aktarımıyla: "I took it just way over the top" [BL]. Blum yazıda "Eshelman expressed regret to me" diyor.
- Abu Ghraib üzerine, 2011 [SM]:
  - "When the Abu Ghraib scandal broke, my first reaction was, this is so familiar to me. I knew exactly what was going on. I could picture myself in the middle of that and watching it spin out of control."

  - En yakını yukarıdaki pişmanlık cümlesi: korkuttuğunu ve zarar verip vermediğini düşünmediğini kabul ediyor.
  - Senaryo bu sözü uydurmamalı; dengeyi "I definitely regret that" kurar.
- 2024'te çerçevesi değişiyor: suçu Zimbardo'nun yönlendirmesine kaydırıyor [LP]. Senaryo ikisini de tarihiyle verebilir.

## 4. Zimbardo'nun yönlendirme toplantısındaki sözleri

**DOĞRU.** Tarih ve kaynak: Cumartesi 14 Ağustos 1971, "Tape 2" dökümü [T2]; ayrıca *Quiet Rage* (Musen, 1992, 5:07–5:44) ve *Lucifer Effect* s. 55 [LT s. 5].

- **Dökümden [T2 s. 2–3]:**
  - "Nevertheless we can create boredom; we can create a sense of frustration; we can create a sense of fear in them, to some degree; we can create a notion of arbitrariness that their life is totally controlled by us, by the system, you, me, Jaffe and so they'll have no privacy at all."
  - "We're going to take away their individuality in various ways. They're going to be wearing uniforms and at no time will anybody call them by name"
  - "In general what all of this leads to is a sense of powerlessness, that is, we have total power in the situation and they have none"
  - "I got a grant to study how the conditions which lead to mob behavior, violence, loss of identity, feelings of anonymity." (T2 s. 3; Le Texier "Tape 2, pp. 2–3")
  - "And we want to recreate in our prison that psychological environment." (T2 s. 2)
  - Süre: "It can run as much as 2 weeks … If it looks like it's too heavy we might have to end earlier." (T2 s. 3–4)
- **Film sürümünde** Blum'un aktardığı ek cümle: "We cannot physically abuse or torture them" [BL]. Bu cümleyi dökümün okuduğum sayfalarında görmedim.

## 5. Zimbardo'nun savunması (2018) [Z18]

**DOĞRU.**
- Talimatlar: "My instructions to the guards, as documented by recordings of the guard orientation, were that they could not hit the prisoners but could create feelings of boredom, frustration, fear, and 'a sense of powerlessness—that is, we have total power of the situation, and they have none.' We did not give any formal or detailed instructions about how to be an effective guard."
- Jaffe kaydı için: "Asking a person role-playing a guard in a prison simulation to be 'firm' and 'in the action' is mild compared to the pressure exerted by actual wardens and superior officers in real-life prison and military settings"
  - "Although the research team asked all guards to be actively involved and firmly in control of the prisoners, it never instructed guards to employ brutality, and it explicitly banned the use of physical force."
- Farklı gardiyanlar: "One or two guards on each shift became progressively meaner over time, others maintained a more even-tempered style, and a few were considered 'good guards' from the prisoners' perspectives. However, none of the 'good guards' ever intervened to prevent the cruelty of their fellow guards."
  - "The fact that some guards remained 'good guards' throughout the study shows that cruel guards chose to act on their own initiative."
- Eshelman için: "I think it goes without saying that such actions go far beyond simply playing the role of a tough guard."
- Kendi kusuru: "In my view, that is the major flaw of the SPE; it had no independent scientific observer of the unfolding events."
- Ana tez: "none of these criticisms present any substantial evidence that alters the SPE's main conclusion"
- **Yön notundaki "sert olmaları söylendi ama nasıl olacakları söylenmedi":** DOĞRU, ama Zimbardo'nun kelimeleriyle değil, Reicher, Haslam ve Van Bavel'in özetiyle: "yes, Guards were told to be tough, but not how to be tough" [PSY].
  - Zimbardo'nun kendi cümlesi: "We did not give any formal or detailed instructions about how to be an effective guard."
- **Le Texier'nin karşı cevabı:** "The evidence presented above directly contradicts this statement." [LT s. 7]
- **2018 sonrası, Blum'a:**
  - "If he wants to say it was all a hoax, that's up to him. I'm not going to defend it anymore. The defense is its longevity." [BL]
  - "The prison study, the negative part is I am Dr. Evil." [BL]

## 10. "Gardiyanların yaklaşık üçte biri" iddiası

**DOĞRU, iki yerde, ikisi de Zimbardo'nun kendi metni.**
- **1973, akademik özet** [HBZ73 s. 69]: "At least a third of the guards were judged to have become far more aggressive and dehumanising toward the prisoners than would ordinarily be predicted in a simulation study."
  - Aynı özet: "Neither group received any specific training in these roles."
- **Prisonexp** [PX /conclusion]: "There were three types of guards. First, there were tough but fair guards who followed prison rules. Second, there were 'good guys' who did little favors for the prisoners and never punished them. And finally, about a third of the guards were hostile, arbitrary, and inventive in their forms of prisoner humiliation."
- **Fromm 1973'te aynı veriyi tersinden okudu** (Le Texier'nin aktarımı) [LT s. 2]: "two thirds of the guards did not commit sadistic acts for personal 'kicks,' the experiment seems rather to show that one can not transform people so easily into sadists by providing them with the proper situation" (Fromm, *The Anatomy of Human Destructiveness*, s. 57–58).
  - Başlık sorusuna en doğrudan veri bu ikili.
- **Zimbardo'nun 2018 metninde "üçte bir" yok.** Orada: "One or two guards on each shift became progressively meaner" [Z18].
