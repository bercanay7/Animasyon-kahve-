Turkish narration with English subtitles: turn on CC (English). On-screen text is in Turkish.

Akira Kurosawa's Ran (1985) and Shakespeare's King Lear: does what you do come back to you one day? Do you reap what you sow?

A castle is burning. An old man walks out of it: no sword, no men, no one at his side.

Ran is King Lear carried over to Japan. But Kurosawa added something Shakespeare never gave the story: a bloody past for the king. The legend of the three arrows, the gates that close one by one, the burning third castle, and the director who painted that castle scene years before he could shoot it and finished the film at 75. Then blind Tsurumaru's flute, and three people who suffered the same wound and chose very different paths: Kaede, Sue, Tsurumaru.
